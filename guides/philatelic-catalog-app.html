StampManage Identifier

A philatelic catalog app for country, category, and topic.

Collectors need more than a photo roll. They need structure that matches how stamp collections are reviewed.

StampManage Identifier supports catalog-style organization so you can browse by country, category, and topic while keeping important details inside each stamp record.

Useful catalog views

  • Country and era for traditional album organization.
  • Category and denomination for issue-level research.
  • Topic for themes such as art, transportation, communication, or famous people.
  • Reports for reviewing collection groups without scrolling item by item.

How to get started

Begin with your most uncertain stamps. Scan, confirm, save, and use topics or categories to keep the catalog useful as it grows.
