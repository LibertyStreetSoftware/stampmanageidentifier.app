Rarity clues to record

  • Country, issue year, denomination, and design type.
  • Color shade, overprint, cancellation, and visible variety.
  • Condition issues such as tears, thinning, heavy cancellation, or poor centering.
  • Whether the stamp is part of a set, error, or limited issue.
