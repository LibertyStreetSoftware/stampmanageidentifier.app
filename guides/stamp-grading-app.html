StampManage Identifier

Track stamp condition before you trust a value estimate.

Identification tells you what the stamp is; grading notes help explain what it may be worth.

StampManage Identifier helps you keep condition, rarity, and value notes attached to each stamp record. That context matters when comparing similar issues or deciding what needs closer review.

Condition details to note

  • Centering, perforations, gum, color, and cancellation strength.
  • Tears, creases, stains, thinning, fading, or missing corners.
  • Whether the stamp is mint, used, hinged, or part of a set.
  • Any uncertainty that should be checked against a specialist catalog.

How to use the app

Open the stamp detail record, review the identification and rarity section, then add condition notes so future value research has context.
