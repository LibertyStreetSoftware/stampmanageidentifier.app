How to identify a stamp from a picture.

Unknown stamps usually become easier to identify when you capture the right details and keep your research organized.

Use a clear, straight-on photo with the full perforated edge visible. Look for country, denomination, portrait, design subject, color, cancellation, and any year or overprint. StampManage Identifier helps turn that scan into a saved record so you can compare, refine, and keep the result in your collection.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Photograph the stamp in bright, even light.
  2. Scan or search in StampManage Identifier.
  3. Review the suggested country, issue, denomination, year, category, and rarity clues.
  4. Add notes when condition, cancellation, or variety still needs manual review.
  5. Save the stamp to your digital catalog.

Why an app helps

Loose research gets lost quickly. A stamp identifier app keeps the image, match, value range, category, and notes together so every scan improves your collection record.
