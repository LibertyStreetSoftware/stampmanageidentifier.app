StampManage Identifier

Export clean stamp collection reports.

Reports make a collection easier to review, print, discuss, or share with another collector.

StampManage Identifier includes report output for collector-ready summaries. Use reports after scanning and cataloging stamps so your inventory is easier to understand outside the app.

When reports help

  • Reviewing an inherited collection by country or category.
  • Preparing a printout for a meeting, sale, or appraisal conversation.
  • Checking collection gaps or duplicates.
  • Sharing a high-level inventory without handing over an album.

How to use the app

Catalog stamps with consistent details first, then export a report grouped around the fields that matter for your next decision.
